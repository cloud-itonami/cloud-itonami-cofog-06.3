# Business Model: Independent Water Infrastructure Leak-Detection Robotics

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-cofog-06.3`
- COFOG: `06.3` (Water supply)
- Activity: distribution-network leak survey, detection and repair
  recommendation
- Social impact: less non-revenue water loss, lower main-break risk, safer
  drinking-water infrastructure

## Customer

- municipal water utilities
- rural water districts without an in-house leak-detection crew
- private water infrastructure operators (campuses, industrial parks)

## Offer

- scheduled + on-demand distribution-network leak survey
- leak-location report with acoustic/pressure sensor evidence
- prioritized repair-recommendation queue
- water-safety-critical escalation (human sign-off, never automated)
- non-revenue-water audit export for utility rate-case justification

## Revenue

- per-segment survey fee
- utility subscription (recurring leak-survey contract)
- non-revenue-water reduction report package for rate-case / grant
  applications
- compliance/audit export package

## Trust Controls

- repair-priority recommendations require Water Infrastructure Governor
  clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` finding always escalates to human sign-off
  (work near live pressurized mains is never automated)
- every survey, finding and escalation is logged
- public leak/loss claims must reference measured sensor data
